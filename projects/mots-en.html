MOTS Memory of the Soul

MOTS turns real conversations into a controlled persona memory. A two-stage AI pipeline first creates a letter, then anchors follow-up dialogue to structured evidence for a brief and restrained emotional experience.

Gemini 2.5 FlashPersona JSONSafety by Design
Problem

Emotional AI may sound familiar without being trustworthy.

Generic chatbots tend to rely on templates, drift in personality or prolong conversation without limits. MOTS narrows the goal to a one-time letter and a small number of follow-up replies grounded in language supplied by the user. It is explicitly not a recreation of a person or a substitute for grief support.

Product Decisions

Key trade-offs

Why not use one prompt?

A single prompt must extract, write and role-play at once, which encourages unsupported completion. Stage one instead produces an inspectable Persona JSON; stage two can answer only within that structured memory.

Why no account?

This is an emotional, low-frequency use case. Registration adds abandonment and privacy anxiety, so the MVP keeps only task-essential information and places model calls and keys on the server.
