02 · WHY THIS PRODUCT

The bottleneck was workflow fragmentation, not copywriting.

The PRD started from repeated account switching, manual time conversion, media rework, fragile publishing and spreadsheet reporting. The MVP was deliberately narrowed to one operating loop.

01

Scattered accounts

Nine channels with fragmented ownership, region and authorization states.

02

Scheduling errors

Domestic 18:00 and overseas 10:00 cadence rules were manually enforced.

03

Media rework

Ratios, covers and file limits required repeated adaptation; WeChat covers need a hard 512KB gate.

04

Untraceable publishing

Without preflight, idempotency and receipts, bulk publishing can misfire or duplicate.

05

Manual reporting

Different metric definitions made it hard to connect content, assets and follow-up decisions.

03 · OPERATING LOOP

Six nodes, each with a clear completion test.

Research

Community pain points, audiences, channel norms, history and competitor signals.

Plan

Build a calendar by account, region, product line and frequency.

Generate

Create scripts, captions, hashtags and CTAs per platform.

Adapt

Validate ratio, clarity, file size and covers while preserving originals.

Publish

Preflight, human approval, outbox, idempotency and platform receipts.

Learn

Normalize metrics, detect winning topics and feed the next planning cycle.

04 · SKILLS ARCHITECTURE

Bounded capability contracts, not one universal prompt.

Each Skill defines its trigger, inputs, outputs, hard gates and completion checks. The orchestrator classifies and routes; it does not absorb every responsibility into an opaque conversation.

hiitio-social-ops-orchestrator

Loads account and schedule rules, selects the narrowest Skill and marks missing data as unknown rather than inventing it.

social-calendar-plannerCross-platform scheduling

Applies region timing and 2–3 posts/week rules, then flags conflicts, missing assets and owners.

social-copy-hashtag-writerPlatform copy, tags and CTAs

Creates channel-specific variants and flags absolute or unsupported claims.

social-asset-adapterMedia inspection and adaptation

Reads format, dimensions, size and duration, preserves originals and enforces the cover limit.

social-publish-preflightReadiness and risk gate

Checks account permissions, fields, media, timing, budget and human confirmation.

social-scheduled-publisherSafe outbox and receipts

Defines idempotency, per-platform states, retries and partial-failure isolation.

social-budget-guardCost and retry gate

Estimates generation/API spend, warns at 80%, blocks at 100% and proposes a smaller scope.

social-video-generation-supervisorScript—storyboard—QA—export

Controls visual drift, unsafe scenes, watermarks, retries and mandatory human review.

social-competitor-trackerWatchlists and breakout signals

Detects strong posts and proposes original follow-ups without copying media or brand assets.

social-analytics-dashboardNormalized metrics and reports

Produces Top/Weak analysis and next actions while keeping missing fields visible.

FRIDAYPARTS → HIITIO

FridayParts exposed the risks that appear after content production scales: incorrect products, unsafe visuals, watermark/text failures, uncontrolled retries, model capability assumptions and untraceable publishing. Those lessons became video supervision, budget, preflight and receipt gates in the Hewei system.

I owned the product loop, not just prompt writing.
